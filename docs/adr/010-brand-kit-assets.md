# ADR-010: Brand masters in brand/, served copies in public/

- **Date**: 2026-10-26
- **Status**: Accepted
- **Tags**: design, brand, process

## Context and Problem Statement

The owner finished a brand kit v1: logo variants, favicon and app icons,
social cards, tokens, fonts and guidelines. The site used a text name in the
header, a placeholder "G" favicon and social cards in the site's own type. The
kit needs a home in the repository, and the site needs to serve some of its
files, without the two drifting apart.

## Decision Drivers

- One master for each asset, so a logo is never edited in two places
- The site serves only what it needs, from `public/`
- The palette and type of the site stay as they are (ADR-008)
- Social cards are regenerated by a script, never edited by hand

## Considered Options

1. Keep the kit outside the repository and copy files in by hand
2. Put the whole kit in `public/` so it is served as is
3. Keep masters in `brand/` (not served) and copy the served files to `public/`

## Decision Outcome

Chosen option: **3**. `brand/` holds the masters (SVG, PNG exports, PDFs,
icons, social sources, tokens, fonts and guidelines). `public/` holds only what
the site serves: the favicon and icons at the root, the header logos in
`public/brand/`. `scripts/build-og-images.mjs` reads the logo and the fonts from
`brand/` to render the social cards into `public/images/og/`.

The site keeps its glacial cyan accent and Geist type. The kit's blue
(`#5B9BFF`) and Inter Display appear only in the logo, the icons and the social
cards.

### Consequences

- A master change needs a matching copy in `public/` in the same change, and
  `brand/README.md` lists the pairs. Nothing enforces the copy, so reviewers
  check it.
- `pnpm build:og-images` depends on `brand/fonts` and `brand/logos`. It is still
  run by hand, and its PNGs are still committed.
- `biome` excludes `brand/` because it holds design sources, not code.
- Social cards and the site no longer share a font: the cards use Inter Display,
  the site Geist. This is deliberate and follows the brand guidelines.
